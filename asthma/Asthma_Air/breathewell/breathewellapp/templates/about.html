About Air Pollutants & Sensors

What are PM2.5 and CO?

PM2.5 are fine inhalable particles with diameters that are generally 2.5 micrometers and smaller. These particles can penetrate deep into your lungs and even enter your bloodstream, causing health problems.

Carbon monoxide (CO) is a colorless, odorless gas that can be harmful when inhaled in large amounts. It reduces oxygen delivery to your body's organs and tissues.

Why Are These Pollutants Harmful?

Exposure to high levels of PM2.5 and CO can increase the risk of respiratory diseases, cardiovascular problems, and worsen existing health conditions like asthma.
